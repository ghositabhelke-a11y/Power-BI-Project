# Hr-dashboard
Recommended Structure and Order
1. Project Title & Headline
HR Attrition & Employee Analytics Dashboard
Built an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, satisfaction levels, and overtime trends, enabling data-driven retention strategies.

2.Short Description / Purpose
This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using the IBM HR Analytics dataset. The dashboard helps HR teams understand workforce trends, identify departments and employee groups with higher turnover, and uncover factors influencing attrition such as age, job role, satisfaction, tenure, and overtime. The solution enables organizations to make proactive retention decisions through data-driven insights.

5.Features / Highlights
Business Problem

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges faced by organizations. High turnover increases recruitment costs, affects productivity, and impacts organizational performance. HR teams often struggle to identify which employee groups are at higher risk of leaving and what factors contribute most to attrition.

Goal of the Dashboard

The objective of this dashboard is to:

Monitor employee attrition trends
Identify departments with high turnover
Analyze attrition across different age groups and genders
Evaluate employee satisfaction levels
Understand the impact of overtime on employee retention
Support HR teams in making data-driven retention decisions
Walkthrough of Key Visuals
KPI Cards

Provides a quick overview of key workforce metrics:

Total Employees
Attrition Count
Attrition Rate
Average Tenure
Satisfaction Index
Average Monthly Income
Attrition by Department

Shows employee attrition across departments.

Insight:
Research & Development and Sales departments experience the highest employee turnover.

Attrition by Age Group

Analyzes attrition among different age categories.

Insight:
Employees below 40 years contribute the majority of attrition cases.

Attrition by Gender

Displays employee attrition distribution between male and female employees.

Insight:
Male employees account for a larger share of attrition compared to female employees.

Department vs Job Role Matrix

Highlights attrition trends across job roles within departments.

Insight:
Specific job roles such as Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives show higher turnover rates.

Overtime vs Attrition

Examines the relationship between overtime work and employee attrition.

Insight:
Employees working overtime demonstrate significantly higher attrition compared to employees with regular work schedules.

Interactive Filters

Allows users to dynamically filter data by:

Department
Gender
Attrition Status

This enables deeper analysis and customized reporting.

Business Impact & Insights
Key Findings
Attrition Rate is approximately 16%.
Research & Development has the highest employee turnover.
Employees below 40 years are more likely to leave the organization.
Overtime is strongly associated with increased attrition.
Certain job roles experience significantly higher turnover than others.
Employee satisfaction levels indicate opportunities for engagement and retention improvements.
Business Impact

This dashboard helps HR managers:

Identify high-risk employee groups
Improve workforce retention strategies
Reduce employee replacement costs
Enhance employee satisfaction initiatives
Support data-driven HR decision-making
Improve overall organizational productivity
